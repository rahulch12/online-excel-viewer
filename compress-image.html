Why Compress Photos in Browser?

Online competitive exams and government application portals require strict file dimensions and file size limits (often 20KB for signatures and 50KB for applicant photographs). SheetBox operates directly on your device using the HTML5 Canvas API, guaranteeing instantaneous compression and inspection without uploading your personal documents to any remote server.
