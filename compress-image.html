Compress Images to 50KB, 100KB, or 200KB for Online Portals

Most official application forms—such as government recruitment portals (UPSC, SSC, State PSC), university entrance examinations, passport portals, and visa application websites—impose strict file size limits. Photos are typically restricted to between 20KB and 50KB or 50KB and 100KB, while signatures must often remain strictly under 20KB.

SheetBox provides an instant, client-side photo resizer and compressor that adjusts image dimensions and JPEG quality directly within your device's memory. No waiting in server queues, and your confidential photos or signatures are never uploaded to any remote server.

How to Reduce Photo Size to 50KB or 100KB Online

  1. Choose or Drag Your Photo: Select any JPG, JPEG, PNG, or mobile camera snapshot from your phone or PC.
  2. Pick a Quick Preset: Tap 50 KB - 100 KB for standard application forms, or Under 20 KB for identity signatures.
  3. Fine-Tune with the Quality Slider: Watch the real-time file size badge update as you adjust the slider.
  4. Download Instantly: Click Download Compressed Image to save your optimized photo ready for immediate form submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reduce my photo size to exactly under 50KB?

Upload your image, click the 50 KB - 100 KB preset button, and if the file is still slightly above 50KB, move the quality slider down to around 40% to 50% or lower the Max Width to 600px.

Is my photo or signature safe on SheetBox?

Yes, 100% safe. Processing takes place entirely in local browser memory via HTML5 Canvas. Your files never touch external storage or servers.

Will compressing to 100KB reduce my photo clarity?

SheetBox applies smart bicubic image downsampling. Even when compressed from 5MB down to 100KB, visual sharpness for passport-style faces and formal IDs is cleanly preserved.
