How to Reduce Image Size in KB for Official Job & Exam Portals

Most competitive exam bodies, government recruitment services, and educational portals enforce strict file size thresholds on applicant photos and signatures. When taking pictures from high-resolution smartphone cameras, file sizes commonly range between 2MB and 6MB—far beyond the permissible 20KB to 100KB limits.

SheetBox provides an automated client-side compression pipeline. Utilizing progressive browser-native discrete cosine transform (DCT) quantization, our compressor preserves readable facial contours and text clarity while eliminating unnecessary metadata and heavy chroma sub-samples.

Standard Requirements for Online Application Portals

Before submitting documents online, refer to standard specifications commonly mandated across major portal categories:

Document Type Typical Target Size Accepted Formats Primary Use Case
Applicant Signature 10 KB – 20 KB JPG, JPEG UPSC, SSC, Banking, State PSC portals
Passport Photograph 20 KB – 50 KB JPG, JPEG Job applications, gate entrance passes, student registration
Photo ID / Identity Card 50 KB – 100 KB JPG, PNG, PDF Aadhaar, Voter ID, driving license proofs
Certificate & Marksheets 100 KB – 300 KB JPG, PDF Academic transcripts and degree attestations

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my personal photos uploaded to an external server?

No. SheetBox processes all image manipulation directly inside your local browser memory using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photos, signatures, and identity cards are never sent over the internet or stored on external servers.

Why do government portals reject photos even when they are under 50KB?

Portals often enforce dimension constraints (like 200×230 pixels) alongside file size rules. If your image is distorted, check whether the portal requires specific aspect ratios or strict JPEG formatting.

Does compressing an image to 20KB make it blurry?

Signatures maintain sharp legibility because high-contrast black-and-white lines compress efficiently. For photographs, SheetBox strikes a balance between artifact removal and facial edge sharpness.

Can I convert a PNG image to a 50KB JPG?

Yes. SheetBox automatically converts uploaded PNG or WebP files to optimized standard JPG format upon download to guarantee portal compliance.
