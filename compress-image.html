Why Compress Photos in Browser?

Online competitive exams and government application portals across India require strict file dimensions and maximum file size limits (often 20KB for signatures and 50KB for applicant photographs). SheetBox operates directly on your device using Canvas API, guaranteeing instantaneous compression without uploading your personal documents to any external server.
