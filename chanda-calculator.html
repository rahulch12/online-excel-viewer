How to Transparently Manage Festival & Society Chanda Collections

Collecting festival funds for Ganesh Utsav, Navratri, Durga Puja, or managing housing society building repair contributions often leads to accounting friction. When paper registers or loose notebooks are used, discrepancies arise regarding who has paid, who is pending, and whether the targeted budget covers major expenses such as pandal decoration, idol procurement, lighting, and prasad distribution.

The SheetBox Chanda & Contribution Calculator replaces manual notebook record-keeping with a secure, browser-native budgeting system. All entries are calculated locally in your browser, guaranteeing member confidentiality while generating clear, shareable reports.

Key Features of the Chanda Calculator

  • Dynamic Budget Deficit Tracking: Set your gross committee target. The system recalculates live collections, outstanding deficits, and per-head pending amounts with every entered donor.
  • Equal Split & Flat-Wise Apportionment: Easily estimate per-flat contributions based on society flat counts or unit dimensions.
  • Instant Excel Export: Download formatted .xlsx registers with a single click to share on society WhatsApp groups for complete fiscal transparency.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does SheetBox store our society member names or amounts on a server?

No. All calculations, tables, and Excel file generation happen 100% inside your local browser memory. No names, phone numbers, or contribution amounts are stored on remote servers.

Can I print the collection list for the society notice board?

Yes. Simply click Print List. The tool isolates the clean collection ledger into a print-ready A4 document without showing input buttons or website menus.

Can I open the exported spreadsheet in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. The exported file is standard .xlsx format, fully compatible with MS Excel, LibreOffice, and Google Sheets.
