Complete Financial Accounting for Community & Festival Committees

Managing public contributions for festivals like Ganesh Utsav, Durga Puja, and Navratri, or collecting society repair funds, requires total accounting transparency. When committee members record cash collections and vendor payouts in physical notebooks, receipts get misplaced, leading to auditing disputes during annual general body meetings (AGM).

The SheetBox Chanda & Expense Calculator provides a complete, double-entry financial overview. It balances incoming member contributions directly against real outgoing vendor expenses (decorations, sound, idol procurement, priest honorarium, and catering) entirely in your browser.

Recommended Expense Breakdown for Festival Mandals

Most community committees budget their funds across these core operational buckets:

  • Pandal & Mandap Setup (30%–40%): Structural frames, waterproofing, seating, and floral backdrops.
  • Sound & Illumination (15%–20%): Lighting fixtures, generator sets, and PA audio systems.
  • Murti & Puja Rituals (10%–15%): Clay idol procurement, flowers, priest fees, and daily aarti materials.
  • Prasad & Mahaprasad (15%–20%): Sweets, community catering, and biodegradable plates.
  • Contingency & Emergency Reserves (5%–10%): Municipal permits, security guards, and post-event cleanup.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does SheetBox store our financial records on a server?

No. All calculations, member names, and vendor expenses remain stored strictly in your browser's private local storage. No data is ever transmitted to an external server.

How does the Excel export work?

Clicking Export Full Excel creates a clean .xlsx workbook containing two distinct tabs: one for member collections and another for categorized expenses, ready to share on WhatsApp groups.

What does Net Balance in Hand indicate?

It represents your true liquid balance: Total Paid Collections minus Total Incurred Expenses. If it turns red, the committee is in an operational deficit and must collect remaining pending pledges.
