Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Welcome to SheetBox (accessible at sheetbox.in). We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring your personal files, identity, and browsing experience remain protected.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero Server File Uploads)

All core file operations on SheetBox—including image compression, PDF merging, document conversion, spreadsheet viewing, and invoice generation—are executed exclusively inside your device's browser memory (using client-side JavaScript APIs such as HTML5 Canvas, SheetJS, and jsPDF). Your files, documents, photos, signatures, and spreadsheets are never uploaded, stored, read, or transmitted to any external server or third-party database.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

This website displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on SheetBox.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to SheetBox and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Web Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics to analyze aggregated traffic trends and improve site performance. Google Analytics collects anonymized technical information such as browser type, operating system, pages visited, and session duration. These logs do not contain personally identifiable information (PII).

4. Local Storage

SheetBox uses standard browser localStorage solely to remember your user preferences (such as your Light/Dark mode choice). This data remains strictly stored on your personal device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out directly via email at chauhanr003@gmail.com.
