About SheetBox

Private, Fast, In-Browser Productivity Tools

SheetBox was founded by software tester and automation engineer Rahul Chauhan with a singular mission: to make everyday document, image, and PDF management completely free, instantaneous, and radically private.

Why We Built SheetBox

Most online converters force users to upload personal resumes, bank statements, identification cards, and business bills to remote cloud servers. This exposes users to privacy risks, queue delays, and restrictive paywalls.

SheetBox replaces cumbersome server-side architectures with modern client-side browser engines. All file manipulation happens locally on your computer or smartphone using WebAssembly and HTML5 APIs.

Our Core Principles

🔒 Privacy First

Zero cloud uploads. Your documents, photos, and spreadsheets never leave your device.

⚡ Zero Latency

No waiting in processing queues. Conversions, compression, and calculations happen in real-time.

🆓 100% Free & Accessible

No credit cards, no logins, and no hidden subscriptions. Professional utilities accessible to students, job seekers, and freelancers worldwide.
