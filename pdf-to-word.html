Free Online PDF to Word (.docx) Converter

Convert native PDF documents into editable Microsoft Word (.docx) documents in seconds. Unlike traditional conversion websites that require you to upload private contracts, financial invoices, or personal resumes to their servers, SheetBox runs parsing algorithms entirely inside your browser memory.

How to Convert PDF to Editable Word Online

  1. Choose or drop your PDF: Select any standard text PDF file from your phone, laptop, or desktop.
  2. Review & Edit in Real-Time: Your PDF text stream is parsed and loaded into the built-in editor, allowing you to proofread, fix typo lines, or delete excess headings.
  3. Download as Word: Click Download as Word (.docx) to compile the structured text into a native document file compatible with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and WPS Office.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents stored on your servers?
No. Every step of the extraction and file construction occurs inside your browser. No data ever leaves your device.

Does this work for scanned image PDFs?
This tool is designed for digital PDFs that contain selectable text streams. Flat image scans (photos of paper) require optical OCR processing, which will be added in a future update.
